### INTRO — lagu tema "MegaWheel Arena" (±25 detik)
Montase ceria: semua tokoh berkendara bersama di jalan pagi, saling melambai; Rocky melompat; Zippy pamer lalu
tergelincir dan tertawa; Buster membunyikan klakson; Sprinkles "ding-ding"; Kraggor mengintip lucu dari balik bukit
lalu bersembunyi; diakhiri **logo MEGAWHEEL ARENA** dan judul episode.

### Scene 1 — "Malam Itu" (kait pembuka)
*Arena, malam, hujan rintik (lembut). Letterbox.*
- *ECU mata Sprinkles, basah oleh hujan.*
- NARRATOR: "Everyone said she was too slow. Too heavy. Too... sweet."
- *Low-angle: Sprinkles di garis start, di antara mobil balap yang ramping.*
- ZIPPY (mengejek): "An ice cream truck? In MY race?"
- NITRO (dingin): "She'll be gone before the first turn."
- *Close: Sprinkles menatap lurus ke depan.*
- SPRINKLES (berbisik): "Not this time."
- *Petir. Dari balik tribun, sepasang mata kuning raksasa menyala.*
- NARRATOR: "This is the story of the day Sprinkles changed everything."
- **Judul: SPRINKLES' FIRST RACE**
- NARRATOR: "It all started... one month earlier."
- **KAIT:** mata kuning berkedip, layar gelap.

### Scene 2 — "Ding-ding!"
*Kota, pagi cerah. Poster besar "MEGAWHEEL GRAND RACE — 30 DAYS TO GO! Registration closes TODAY".*
- SPRINKLES (bernyanyi): "Ding-ding! Ice cream for everyone!"
- TILLY (taksi, ceria, cerewet): "Morning, Sprinkles! One strawberry, please. Oh, did you hear? The Grand Race is back!"
- SPRINKLES: "Here you go, Tilly. Extra sprinkles, just for you!"
- TILLY: "They say the winner gets a golden trophy THIS big!"
- *Sprinkles berhenti di depan poster. Close mata berbinar.*
- SPRINKLES (pelan): "Thirty days..."
- BUSTER (datang): "You're staring at that poster again, Sprinkles."
- SPRINKLES (malu): "Just looking, Buster. Just... looking."
- BUSTER (hangat): "You've been 'just looking' for ten years."
- **KAIT:** close-up tulisan kecil di poster: "Registration closes TODAY." Mata Sprinkles membesar.

### Scene 3 — "Booth Pendaftaran"
*Arena, siang. Booth pendaftaran: panggung kecil, tenda, papan "RACE REGISTRATION" di dua tiang, meja, bendera.*
- SPRINKLES (gugup): "One... one entry, please."
- *Hening. Lalu tawa. Zippy dan Nitro terbahak.*
- ZIPPY: "Ha! What's next? A shopping cart?"
- NITRO: "This is a race, not a picnic."
- SIREN (tegas): "That's enough. Rules are rules. Anyone with four wheels can race."
- SIREN (lembut, ke Sprinkles): "Name?"
- SPRINKLES: "Sprinkles. Just... Sprinkles."
- *Close: Sprinkles menahan air mata, mengangguk.*
- ZIPPY (berbisik ke Nitro): "Bet she won't even reach the first turn."

### Scene 4 — "Malam yang Sepi"
*Garasi, malam. Satu lampu hangat. Foto Grandpa Cone di dinding.*
- SPRINKLES (sendiri, sedih): "Maybe they're right. I'm just an ice cream truck."
- *Ia mencoba ngebut di dalam garasi, menabrak tumpukan kardus. Wafer berjatuhan.*

- *Close: foto Grandpa Cone tersenyum.*
- SPRINKLES (berbisik): "Grandpa... what would you do?"
- *Air mata jatuh. Lampu berkedip lembut.*
- **KAIT:** foto memudar ke warna sepia. NARRATOR: "...and she started to remember."

### Scene 5 — "Janji Kecil" (kilas balik, MANY YEARS AGO)
*Tepi lintasan lama, senja, sepia, butir film. Little Sprinkles (kecil, suara anak) & Grandpa Cone.*
- LITTLE SPRINKLES: "Grandpa! Look how fast they go!"
- GRANDPA CONE: "Fast is nice, little one. But do you know what makes a real champion?"
- LITTLE SPRINKLES: "Winning?"
- GRANDPA CONE: "No. A champion is the one who stops... to help someone else."
- LITTLE SPRINKLES: "Even if they lose the race?"
- GRANDPA CONE: "Especially then. Trophies get dusty. Kindness never does."
- LITTLE SPRINKLES: "Then one day, I'll race. And I'll be that kind of champion!"
- GRANDPA CONE: "I know you will."
- **KAIT:** kembali ke masa kini, subuh. Sprinkles membuka pintu garasi: "Tomorrow, I start training." Kalender: **29**.

### Scene 6 — Latihan 1: "Pelan-pelan" (pelajaran: sabar)
*Jalan pedesaan, pagi. Kalender 29 → 25.*
- *Sprinkles langsung tancap gas: es krim beterbangan, ia tergelincir dan berputar.*
- SPRINKLES: "Whoa-whoa-WHOA!"
- BUSTER (sabar): "Easy, easy. Why are you rushing?"

- BUSTER: "Then don't try to be them. Listen to your engine. Start slow... then smooth."
- *Sprinkles mencoba lagi: pelan, halus, lebih jauh dari sebelumnya.*

- BUSTER: "Slow is smooth. Smooth is fast."
